Transcription is one of the few categories where open source wins outright. The models are good, they run on ordinary machines, and the audio never leaves your computer — which matters if you're transcribing an interview or a client meeting.

Verdict

For most people: Whisper large-v3 and be done with it. Fewest problems in Portuguese and it runs on modest hardware.

If you transcribe for a living and the volume is high, setting up Parakeet is worth the work.

📷 Screenshot of the test (replace with your own image)

Which for what

  • Whisper large-v3 — the all-rounder. If you want one choice and no more thinking, this is it. Handles Portuguese well.
  • NVIDIA Parakeet TDT — fastest for volume. Hours of audio in one go is where this wins.
  • NVIDIA Canary-Qwen 2.5B — most accurate in English. Not the obvious pick for Portuguese.
  • Moonshine — built to run on-device, where there is no cloud. Phone, Raspberry Pi, offline.

Why this matters more than it looks

Commercial transcription services charge per minute and receive your audio. For a public interview that's fine. For a client meeting, a consultation, or a recording with personal data, it's a privacy decision — and GDPR doesn't like ambiguity there.

Running the model locally solves both at once: cost and privacy.
