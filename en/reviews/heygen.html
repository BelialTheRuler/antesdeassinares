Videos with a virtual presenter solve a lot: training, marketing, sales, all without a camera and without showing your face. But two names come up in every conversation, HeyGen and Synthesia, and picking between them isn't obvious. I used both for a month on paid accounts, and my answer is simpler than most articles make it seem.

Verdict

If you work alone or in a small team and want quality sales videos and translation at the best price, HeyGen is the obvious pick.

If you need approvals, SSO and brand consistency for a large team, Synthesia justifies its price. For everyone else, HeyGen does the job for less.

Quick summary

  • HeyGen — for solo work: sales videos, translation, individual use. More flexible and cheaper.
  • Synthesia — for teams and companies: templates, approval flows, SSO. Heavier, but more professional.
My rule of thumb: solo creator, go HeyGen. Company with processes and a team, go Synthesia.

Where they actually differ

  • Avatars: both have hundreds, and both support custom avatars. HeyGen makes it easier to clone yours from a short video.
  • Languages: both speak Portuguese. I find HeyGen slightly more natural.
  • Price: HeyGen starts lower, around $29/month, with a free watermarked plan. Synthesia also starts at $29, but the tier that matters for companies costs more.
  • Input: HeyGen accepts a URL, PowerPoint or plain text. Synthesia has a more polished editor, but a more closed one.
  • Translation: HeyGen wins easily. It translates and keeps your voice.
