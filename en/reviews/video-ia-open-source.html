Video is the category where open source is most work and where commercial still wins most often. Worth understanding why before you spend a weekend installing things.

Verdict

If you need a talking avatar, pay. Open source doesn't do that well yet and you'll lose more in time than you save in subscription.

If you need generated shots, b-roll or unlimited experimentation, the open models win — and past twenty videos a month, they win comfortably.

The five

  • LTX-2.5 — among the fastest to generate and the easiest to get running.
  • Wan — good balance between quality and hardware demand.
  • HunyuanVideo — high quality, high hardware.
  • CogVideoX — the most studied and documented, a good starting point if you want to understand the internals.
  • Mochi 1 — more natural motion, but heavy.

What commercial still does better

Avatars that speak with your face and voice, translation with lip sync, and template libraries. That's why a tool like HeyGen still makes sense for solo creators — we compared the two big ones in HeyGen vs Synthesia.

Open models are better at something else: generate whatever you want, no monthly cap, and no terms of service telling you what you may film.
