Turning text into audio is easy these days. The problem shows up when you need more than that: editing the narration, syncing with the slides, adding music underneath. Murf was built around that problem, for people making presentations, courses and training. I used it for a month, and the verdict is more specific than most reviews admit.

Verdict

If you make presentations, e-learning, training or corporate videos and want a polished result, voice, music and slides in the same place, Murf makes sense. It's a specific audience, but for it there's no equally complete alternative.

What is Murf AI?

An AI voice platform that goes beyond text-to-speech. It has a multi-track studio where you edit audio, adjust emphasis and pauses, sync with PowerPoint or Google Slides, and add music and effects. Over 130 voices in over 20 languages, Portuguese included.

What I used it for, and how it went

  • Quality: natural and expressive voices. The emphasis and pause controls make a real difference in presentations.
  • Studio: multi-track editor with music, images and transitions. Rare among competitors.
  • Presentations: syncs with PowerPoint and Google Slides. If you teach or present, this is the point.
  • Languages: Portuguese voices are available with good quality, though fewer than English ones.

Pros

  • Full studio: audio, music and slides
  • Excellent for presentations and e-learning
  • Natural voices with emphasis control

Cons

  • Pricier than plain text-to-speech tools
  • Fewer PT voices than EN
  • The studio has a learning curve
  • Paid plans needed for commercial use

Pricing (2026)

  • Free: 10 minutes of generation a month. Enough to get the idea.
  • Creator: ~$29/month, ~$19 with annual billing. The most popular plan.
  • Business: ~$49/month, for teams and commercial use.
  • Enterprise: on request.
For presentations and training, Creator is more than enough. Only move up to Business for team work or wider commercial use.

Alternatives

  • ElevenLabs — better raw voice quality and more PT voices, without the studio (we reviewed it).
  • Play.ht — simpler and cheaper, no studio.
  • Free: Edge TTS (Windows) for quick tests.
