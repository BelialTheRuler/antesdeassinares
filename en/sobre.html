Who we are and how we test

Who we are

We're a small project that tests AI tools and publishes honest reviews and guides. We don't affiliate with anyone we haven't used — and we tell you when a tool is not worth it, even if that means less commission.

How we test

  • We test with real accounts (free or paid) — never just demos.
  • We publish pros and cons, including what went wrong.
  • We update pricing and verdicts regularly.
  • We separate opinion from fact: if it's opinion, we say so.

Affiliate policy (disclosure)

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we earn a commission — at no extra cost to you.

  • Every article states at the top that it contains affiliate links.
  • Affiliate links never change the price you pay.
  • We never recommend a tool just because it pays a commission.
  • When there are competing programs, we present both with their own links.

Privacy

This site doesn't load third-party trackers without consent. We only use local storage to remember your preferences — as the cookie banner shows. If we ever add traffic analytics, we'll ask for consent first.
